How to take part

Two tasks at once. Keep your eyes at the center.

While watching the central digits, also remember a letter that appears off to the side. You will answer about that letter after the display disappears. The task fills the screen with a gray background.

01

Catch every zero

Before each trial, look at the central + for one second. The digits then start automatically. Press Space whenever you see 0. Do not respond to the other digits.

Keep looking at the center throughout the display.

02

Remember the letter and its color

A group of four letters briefly appears above or below, to the left or right of the center. One is T, X, or L; the other three are the letter O.

Keep looking at the center and remember which letter (T, X, or L) appeared and what color it was. Ignore the O’s, including the colored O.

After the display disappears, nine answer buttons appear. Choose the button showing the same letter in the same color. One choice reports both.

Answer at your own pace. Click the matching button or press the number printed on it. If unsure, give your best answer.
